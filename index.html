About me

Huikang (Will) Ye

PhD candidate · Cancer Biology and Oncology

University of Pittsburgh · Chen Lab

I work at the intersection of machine learning, cancer biology, and drug discovery. My research focuses on predicting drug responses and prioritizing compounds for cancer cells associated with treatment resistance and metastatic potential.

I develop computational workflows that connect chemical information, biological context, and experimental screening data, working closely with experimental researchers to guide follow-up studies.

My background also includes a Master of Management of Innovation at the University of Toronto. Business education is an important part of my training, alongside experimental biology and computation.
